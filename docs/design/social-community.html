halalfood.world
Design proposal · social community

Find halal food through the people you trust

These are the screens and flows halalfood.world is missing to become a social community around halal food. They are modelled on Corner's friend feed, map, lists and visit logging. The brand, the evidence-first halal statuses and the moderation rules stay exactly as they ship today. The social layer sits on top of them.

Taste is social, halal status is not

Friends' likes, lists and streaks decide what to try. Halal status still comes only from dated, moderated evidence. No follower count or like can move a badge.

Every visit can carry a halal check

Corner's "been" log gains three optional questions (certificate seen, alcohol, slaughter). A friend's post feeds the existing moderation queue as well as the feed.

Your standard filters everything

The dietary standard picked in onboarding (the existing preferences) filters the feed, the map, lists and recs. Nobody is shown a place that fails their own rules.

Rank by verified visits, not by volume

Leaderboards and curator badges count verified check-ins, following the existing anti-manipulation rules. They never reward raw volume.

Navigation

Keep the shipped bottom bar, its orange active state and labels. Explore becomes Home, with the feed on top of the existing rails. Saved and Passport move into You. Corner's floating plus becomes our raised Add, which now opens a sheet.

Runs after the first email-code login. Six short steps, and each can be skipped except name and handle. It ends on the user's own map, as Corner's does.

The MapLibre map at /map gains Corner's "whose places" filter and friend-face pins. The status and cuisine filters from map-filters stay as chips.

4 · Log a visit

This is the loop that powers the feed. Corner's one-screen "been" sheet is extended with the existing structured halal check, which stays optional and collapsed by default. A logged visit becomes a verified visit, a check-in with dishes, an optional moderated halal submission and a feed post.

5 · Save from reels

Corner's headline feature. We already turn Instagram, TikTok and YouTube links into creator videos through oEmbed. This flow adds place matching and saves to "Want to try". On mobile, the app's share-sheet extension makes it one tap from inside Instagram.

Corner: save spots from reels

6 · Lists

Lists already exist (placeLists, /lists, /list/[id]). Corner adds covers, discovery, collaboration, saves and a "you've been" counter.

Corner: lists for youlist detail + collaboratesave list on map

7 · Profiles

Your own profile merges Saved, Lists and the food passport. Someone else's profile adds follow and "in common". Public profiles stay reachable at /u/[handle] and keep the existing visibility rules.

Corner: profile with streak + statssaved lists + recent places

8 · Community

The existing /leaderboard, reworked in Corner's style: city or global, this week or all time, and friendlier cards. Friends are found from the same place.

Corner: leaderboardtop curatorsfind friends

9 · Search

/search today covers places and cities. It gains People and Lists tabs. Results pages stay server-rendered for SEO.

Corner: place result with featured-on and curators · The empty state follows common search patterns; Mobbin has no Corner screen for it.

10 · Activity and recs

Corner's bell and paper plane. The bell carries social events and one alert only we can send: a halal status changed at a place you saved. The plane sends a place or list to a friend. We don't build open chat.

Corner shows the bell and plane on every home screen (reference). Mobbin doesn't capture the screens behind them, so these follow common activity-feed patterns.

11 · Events

Corner's "Coming up" lists club nights. Ours lists halal food events: iftar walks, Eid markets and food festivals. Every vendor carries its own status, because a festival being "halal" is not one fact.

Build plan

Ordered so each phase ships something usable on its own. New tables go in apps/web/src/db/schema.ts. Rules with no DOM dependency go in packages/core so the future Expo app shares them.

Phase 1

Social graph and onboarding

  • follows (follower, followee, status for private accounts), blocks
  • Onboarding steps 1–6, with the standard written to userPreferences
  • Profile avatar, name and handle on userProfiles
  • Invite links. Contacts matching comes with the mobile app.
Phase 2

Log a visit and the feed

  • A visit gets verdict and note on placeVisits / placeCheckIns
  • A feed_events fan-out on read, filtered by the viewer's standard and blocks
  • reactions, comments, both reportable through contentReports
  • Weekly streak rule in core/streaks.ts, Ramadan-aware
Phase 3

Lists, map, search

  • list_collaborators, list_saves, list cover and caption
  • Map "whose places" filter and friend-avatar pins
  • Search tabs for people and lists
  • Reel paste with place matching, reusing placeMediaLinks
Phase 4

Activity, recs, events

  • notifications, including status-changed alerts from placeHalalStatusHistory
  • recs (sender, recipient, place or list, note), with no free chat
  • events and event_vendors linked to places
  • Leaderboard windows: this week and all time

References are the Corner iOS screens on Mobbin, linked in each section. Place names, handles and numbers in these mockups are examples. Brand tokens, type and the placeholder photo treatment come unchanged from packages/ui and apps/web/src/components.
