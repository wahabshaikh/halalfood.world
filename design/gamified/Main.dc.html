The three directions
A · Mizan the ledger
Warm paper, Instrument Serif over DM Sans, hairline rules. Gamification reads as a record, not a game: standing, seals, a contribution ledger. Closest to today's site.
B · Suhoor the night market
Near-black with lantern amber and mint, Archivo over Space Grotesk. Leagues, seasons, streak multipliers, promotion zones, a live city feed. Loudest of the three.
C · Zellige the passport
Cream, teal and saffron, Syne over Karla, 2px ink borders and hard offset shadows. Verification is four tiles you turn; contributions collect into city passports and stamps.
