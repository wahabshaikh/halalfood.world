# Design specificity verdict

**Four of five surface types are category-interchangeable. The product's differentiator exists on exactly one page type.**

The three design reviews that looked at list surfaces reached this independently, and the database proves it is a design omission rather than a data limit: there are **66 approved halal verifications** seeded, and **7 of London's 15 places are `evidence-backed` while 8 are `unverified`**. The map, `/cities`, `/city/[slug]` and `/guides/[slug]` render **zero** of that distinction. Every row on every list surface carries the same three constants:

- `HALAL LISTING · {city}` — `src/components/place-list.tsx:32`, `app/food-map.tsx:126`
- `Halal place` tile caption — `place-list.tsx:28`, `food-map.tsx:123`
- `HALAL LISTING` in the map's selected-place preview, in a class literally named `selected-preview-evidence` — `food-map.tsx:180`

None derive from anything. Rows are selected by `halal_confirmed = 1` (`src/lib/places.ts:63`) — the flag `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/product/evidence-first-halal-status.md` both state **is not a public halal status**. So the highest-traffic pages take a private publication flag, relabel it "HALAL LISTING", and print it on all 60 rows.

The formatter that produces the honest labels already exists, client-safe and dependency-free, at `src/lib/halal-status-view.ts:91-108`. Exactly one page imports it.

Strip the word "halal" from four strings and this ships as a generic directory tomorrow. Meanwhile the only authority visible on a card is a borrowed one — `★ 4.30 Google · 1,645`, rendered at higher contrast, larger size and heavier weight than the halal string above it. A product built to argue that borrowed trust signals aren't good enough has made Google's star rating the most prominent trust signal on its own home screen.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 findings in source (`app/globals.css:1` overused-font, `:2122` layout-transition — zero findings in any `.tsx`). 20 live URL scans returned **1,074 findings**, every scan exiting 2. These resolve to far fewer distinct issues: 387 low-contrast instances are **7 colour pairs**, and two token values cause **97%** of them.

# What's working

**1. The fail-closed status derivation is unusually disciplined, and it is a design asset.** `mapHalalStatus` (`src/lib/halal-status.ts:54-74`) refuses to guess: a non-integer count, a zero count with a stray timestamp, an approved count with an unparseable date each return `unavailable` rather than degrading to a friendlier answer. `formatHalalStatus` re-checks and *downgrades* an evidence-backed status if it cannot format the date. Three independent layers refuse to overstate. That is Product Principle 1 implemented in code, not just in copy, and it is rare.

**2. The URL/deep-link contract is real and carefully built.** `food-map.tsx:342-351, 415-451` strips `city`/`lat`/`lng`/`z` on selection, writes `?place=<id>` via `replaceState`, reads and validates them on cold load, and `jumpTo`s before selecting with `fly: false` so there is no disorienting animation. Every map state is shareable. Combined with server-rendered listings, real `<a href>` on every control, `ItemList`/`BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD, correct canonicalisation of paged views, and `robots: noindex` on degraded renders, this is the most disciplined thinking in the product.

**3. The section-level copy is genuinely editorial and earns trust.** "Show the source behind the halal claim." "Share a visit, not a score." "A shortlist is a starting point, not a verdict." "Personal impressions are directional, not certification or a score." Plus the approximate-location disclosure — an amber banner above the fold on every listing page volunteering the weakness of the product's own coordinate data. Almost no directory does that.

**4. The leaderboard's scoring is inspectable and on-brand.** `app/leaderboard/page.tsx:118-125` renders the live `CONTRIBUTOR_SCORE_WEIGHTS` constants rather than a hard-coded sentence, so the strip cannot drift from the algorithm. A row was recomputed by hand and reconciles exactly. Saves are deliberately excluded — the board rewards giving, not hoarding.

# Priority issues

## P0 — The evidence chain is invisible on 4 of 5 surface types
**What.** Detailed above. `findPlacesByCity` (`src/lib/places.ts:283-290`) and `/api/places` never join `place_halal_verifications`. No filter or sort by evidence status exists anywhere in the product.
**Why it matters.** Violates Product Principles 1 and 2 on the pages that receive the most first-time traffic, and re-introduces the exact unconditional claim the evidence-first spec exists to abolish. The one thing a competitor cannot copy is the one thing a visitor never sees.
**Fix.** Add a grouped `LEFT JOIN` on approved verifications to `findPlaces`/`findPlacesByCity` (one subquery, no N+1, no migration). Replace the constant kicker with the derived status — `Evidence-backed · 3 reviewed` or `Not yet reviewed`, neutral, never red. Add an `Evidence-backed` filter chip. Differentiate map markers: filled for evidence-backed, hollow for unverified. Delete the `Halal place` tile caption.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify` then `/impeccable layout`

## P0 — The unconditional halal claim ships in the meta description, beside the "Unverified" badge
**What.** `src/lib/seo.ts:115` builds `"{name} is a halal {cuisine} restaurant in {where}."` unconditionally. **Verified live** on an unverified place:
```
name="description" content="Bab Al Yemen is a halal Indian restaurant in London. Rated 4.30. …"
```
The same string renders in `<p class="place-summary">` directly beneath a badge reading *"Unverified — No halal evidence has been reviewed yet."*
**Why it matters.** The honest badge stays on the page; the unconditional claim travels to Google, WhatsApp and every shared link. Truthfulness is supposed to outrank reassurance; right now reassurance has wider distribution.
**Fix.** Pass the resolved `HalalStatus` into `placeDescription`. Unverified → *"…listed for halal diners. No halal evidence has been reviewed yet."* Render "Halal evidence shown below" only when `approvedCount > 0`.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## P0 — A basemap failure permanently deadlocks the listings, with an undismissable toast
**What.** `food-map.tsx:453` gates place loading on `ready`, set only by MapLibre's `load` event (`:393`). Reproduced by aborting only `basemaps.cartocdn.com`: **`/api/places` was called 0 times in 20 seconds** (control: 2 calls, 3 cards from 5s). The panel reads "Finding places… / Loading places…" unchanged at 2s, 5s, 10s, 15s, 20s. The `Retry` branch (`:700`) renders only when `error` is set, which this path never sets — and the toast's Dismiss handler clears `notice` while the text comes from `mapError`, so **the toast cannot be dismissed either**. Meanwhile typing in the search box successfully calls `/api/places/search` and returns 24 results: the page proves its own data source is healthy while claiming to be loading.
**Why it matters.** On hotel wifi or a captive portal blocking the tile CDN, the product is a grey rectangle forever. Architecturally the product treats the basemap as the source of truth for its own data; the list is the product and the map is a view of it.
**Fix.** Run `loadPlaces` on mount against `viewFromParams(params) || DEFAULT_VIEW`, independent of `ready`. Add an 8s watchdog that sets `mapError` and falls back to a list-first layout. Fix the dismiss handler to clear `mapError`.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

## P0 — `/add` server-renders no form at all
**What.** **Verified:** `curl /add` returns exactly one `<h1>Checking your sign-in…</h1>` and **zero** occurrences of "Add this place". The whole form is gated behind a `fetch("/api/auth/get-session")` with no timeout and no AbortController (`add-place-form.tsx:51-70`) — if that request hangs rather than rejects, the page stays on that sentence permanently. Then the default tab is "Search with Google", which is auth-gated server-side and returns 401 to a signed-out visitor: **the first action on the contribution page is a refusal.**
**Why it matters.** This is the supply line for the evidence chain. `PRODUCT.md` names no-JS behaviour as incumbent; this page breaks it.
**Fix.** Server-render the form in its signed-out configuration (manual mode, draft-preserve submit) and let the session check *upgrade* it. Default `mode` to `"manual"` when signed out. Add a 5s timeout falling through to signed-out.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

## P0 — The OTP error instructs an action the screen cannot perform
**What.** `login-form.tsx:164`: *"That code is not valid or has expired. Request a new one and try again."* **There is no resend control on that step.** The only other button is "Use a different email" (`:244`). Simultaneously `setStatus("")` (`:147`) erases the "We sent a 6-digit code to {email}" line, so the user also loses which address it went to. Neither the 5-minute expiry nor the 3-attempt limit (`auth.ts:88-90`) is ever shown, and the 5-per-24-hours request cap computes `Retry-After` server-side and discards it.
**Why it matters.** This is the terminal step of the activation funnel and it is a literal dead end. Everyone whose code expires while they were in their inbox hits it.
**Fix.** Add "Send a new code" with a cooldown timer. Render the "sent to x@y.com" line as a persistent sub-header, not as `status`. State the expiry and attempts remaining.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

## P1 — 310 of 320 focusable elements are under 44×44, and the most common height is 42px
**What.** Measured across 11 screens: **96.9%** fail. 140 are under 24px in a dimension. `/city/london` and `/guides/london` are **100%** failing, with 38 severe each — 15× `Show on map` at **88×16** and 15 card titles at 19px tall. `/` has `View details` at **83×14**, the smallest on the site, and it is the only route to the evidence chain. The single most common size sitewide is `Nx42` — **2px under the threshold**, so one shared control-height change clears a large fraction.
Compounding it: `.page-nav { display: none }` below 680px (`globals.css:2286`) with **no hamburger, no drawer, no bottom bar** on any page except `/`. Cities, Guides, Community and Saved are unreachable from the header on phones.
**Fix.** `min-height: 44px` on `.action`, `.card-link`, `.page-nav-link`, `.pagination a` and the save control. Add a mobile nav below 680px.
**Command:** `/impeccable adapt`

## P1 — The place page spends 5 of its 30-request rate-limit budget on one view
**What.** **Verified:** four components on the place page each fetch `/api/auth/get-session` independently (`place-halal-verification.tsx`, `place-reviews.tsx`, `place-rating.tsx`, `place-photos.tsx`) plus `save-place-button.tsx` — **5 identical calls per load**, out of 11 API requests total. `src/lib/auth.ts:80-84` sets Better Auth to `{ window: 60, max: 30 }` per IP. Controlled 6-visit run: visits 1–3 clean, visit 4 → one 429, visits 5–6 → **all five get-session calls 429**. A user browsing **six place pages in a minute rate-limits their own session endpoint.**
**Fix.** Share one session lookup via context or a module-level promise, as `save-place-button.tsx` already does for saved IDs.
**Command:** `/impeccable optimize`

## P1 — An undrained 401 body stops 4 route families reaching network-idle
**What.** **Verified:** `/api/places/saved` returns 401 with `transfer-encoding: chunked`, and `save-place-button.tsx:28` returns early on `status === 401` **without consuming the body**. The stream never closes, so Chromium never fires `requestfinished`. `/city/*`, `/guides/*`, `/place/*` and `/saved` never reach network-idle — confirmed by a 30s navigation timeout on exactly those four, while curl serves them in 0.18–0.35s.
**Why it matters.** Silently breaks Lighthouse, CWV field tooling and any `networkidle`-based test, and leaves the browser's loading indicator spinning. Invisible to every design review.
**Fix.** `await response.text()` (or `.body?.cancel()`) before the early return, in both `save-place-button.tsx:28` and `saved-places-view.tsx:18`.
**Command:** `/impeccable optimize`

## P1 — Two competing `:root` blocks; the dead palette still supplies live tokens
**What.** `globals.css:3` (green `#17784a`) and `:3168` (terracotta `#b84830`). Block 2 wins **18 of 22** tokens. Block 1 survives on 4 it never declares: **`--coral: #dc2626`**, `--coral-soft`, `--primary-foreground`, `--font-sans`. So a cool crimson error palette authored against green now sits beside terracotta — near-neighbours on the hue wheel. The visible consequence is on `/add`: the encouraging note "You can prepare the listing now…" is painted `--primary-soft`/`--primary-dark`, pink-on-pink, and **reads as a rejection** at the top of the contribution funnel.
Residue: 29 hardcoded colours outside `:root`, 8 of them cool grey-greens from the dead palette; `#fffdf8` retyped 5× instead of using `--card`; `--radius` overridden 16→12px while 8 declarations still hardcode 16px; 23 distinct font sizes with every integer 8–18 present except 19; **38.7% of font-size declarations below 11px**.
**Fix.** Collapse to one `:root`, delete the green block, explicitly define the four orphans, and give errors a non-hue signal (left border + icon) so they don't rely on red-vs-terracotta.
**Command:** `/impeccable extract`

## P2 — Four date formats on one page, one hard-locked to en-US
**What.** `halal-status-view.ts:71` uses `Intl.DateTimeFormat("en-US", …)` → `August 23, 2026`; `place-halal-verification.tsx:310` uses bare `toLocaleDateString()` → `7/24/2026`; `place-photos.tsx:77` → `Aug 26, 2026`; `place-reviews.tsx:74` → date + time. All four render on the same place page, producing a visible contradiction: the panel says "Latest reviewed August 23, 2026" above cards dated 7/22–7/24 with no explanation.
**Why it matters.** For a global product whose proposition is an auditable review date, the status line is the wrong thing to freeze to US format — and the bare call produces the ambiguous `7/24/2026` form.
**Fix.** One shared formatter, visitor locale, explicit labels: `Submitted 22 Jul 2026 · Reviewed 23 Aug 2026`.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## P2 — Assorted, each small and each real
- **`button:disabled { cursor: wait }`** (`globals.css:70-73`) makes every disabled button claim it is loading. The sign-in button renders this way on page load simply because the email field is empty — and because it is `disabled`, it is **not a tab stop**, so keyboard order goes email → 3 unlabelled Turnstile divs → footer, skipping the only submit control.
- **`/login` overflows 32px at 320px.** `section.auth-card` measures 338px wide in a 320 viewport; culprit is Turnstile's fixed 300px iframe plus 18px padding each side. Every form sibling is stretched to that 300px floor. Separately, `div.page-header-actions` overflows 1px at 320 on all 9 non-map pages.
- **Map search is not a combobox.** Results render **24 buttons for 12 places** (the component renders twice, desktop + mobile). `role`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-activedescendant`, `aria-autocomplete` are all null; `ArrowDown` does nothing. The `role="status"` element is removed once results arrive, so **the arrival of 12 results is announced to nobody**. Marker click, by contrast, is `aria-live="polite"` and correct.
- **No skip link on any screen.** `a[href^='#']` returns `[]` everywhere. On `/place` that's 8 header + 2 breadcrumb stops before content.
- **No dark mode.** Zero `prefers-color-scheme` blocks in 3,776 lines, for a product used one-handed at night in unfamiliar cities.
- **No sign-out and no profile anywhere.** **Verified:** `grep -rn "signOut|Sign out|Log out"` across `app` and `src` returns nothing outside `api/auth`. The header account icon links to `/login` unconditionally, so a signed-in contributor clicking it is greeted "WELCOME BACK / Sign in". Their leaderboard name is fixed at signup with no way to change it — including the anonymised `Contributor · 7eca` handle.
- **`unverified` and `unavailable` badges are styled identically** (`globals.css:2511-2521`). "Nobody has checked yet" and "we can't tell you right now" are different facts with different consequences.
- **Out-of-range `?page=` 404s** instead of clamping (`city/[citySlug]/page.tsx:104`) — verified: `/city/london?page=1` returns the generic 404, stripping all London context mid-browse.
- **`/guides/[city]` renders the same places in the same order as `/city/[city]`** via the same `PlaceList` component. Two pages competing for one query, and the "guide" withholds the evidence it tells you to check.
- **Contribution is never emotionally rewarded.** The `/add` success card says "Your place is listed" then immediately hedges with a consumer-facing caution, offers View/Share/Add another, and never mentions evidence, points, or the leaderboard — at the moment of peak motivation, for the person best placed to supply the 8-point evidence submission. The listing they open will read *unverified* seconds after they ticked "I confirm this place is halal".

# Persona red flags

**The traveler who just landed, one hand, hotel wifi.** The H1 never renders on her device (`.explore-rail { display: none }`). The provenance line never renders (`.map-bottom-note { display: none }`). The bottom nav covers the collapsed sheet's place count and its `See places` button, so the two things she needs in three seconds are both occluded. The map opens on a hardcoded Mumbai centroid regardless of where she is; "where am I" is a 40px icon she must find. If the tile CDN is blocked she waits forever. She cannot reach Cities, Guides or Saved from any header.

**The first-timer arriving cold from Google.** Lands on `/city/london`, reads "15 halal restaurants in London… with addresses, phone numbers and ratings", and concludes this is a listings site with a nice serif. The one differentiating sentence is in the footer at 11px and 3.93:1, under a chat bubble on her phone. No "how we verify" link exists in the header or footer nav. The 8 unverified places she is looking at all say `HALAL LISTING`.

**The cautious eater who needs proof.** Reaches the section headed *"This is the strongest trust layer on the page"* and is shown three assertions with `evidence: []` — no attachment, no author (`PublicHalalVerification` has no author field at all), and a date that contradicts the summary four lines above. She cannot tell whether three people checked or one person submitted three times. The word "moderators" appears twice on the page and is never defined.

**The keyboard / screen-reader user.** No skip link anywhere. 96.9% of targets under 44px. Map card buttons announce run-on names — `"MAHalal placeHALAL LISTING · MumbaiMohammed Ali Ro…"` — a decorative monogram and an unqualified halal claim before the restaurant name. `/add`'s tablist has no `aria-controls` and no `role="tabpanel"`, and its success transition destroys the focused element silently. The `/login` error sits *after* the escape-hatch button in DOM order. Leaderboard ranks 1–3 announce as nothing (`<span>` + `aria-hidden` icon). On `/place`, `scroll-behavior: smooth` means focus animates to each target.

**The motivated first-time contributor.** Default tab refuses her. Redirected to a page saying "WELCOME BACK" that never mentions the listing she just typed. Code expires in her inbox; she is told to request a new one by a screen with no such button, having just lost the address it went to. If she gets through, she must press "Add this place" a second time with no explanation. Her reward is a caution and a listing marked *unverified*. Her public name is `Contributor · 7eca` and there is no screen where she can change it.
